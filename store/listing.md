## Name

## Category

## Short description (max 132 chars)
Instantly check whether the LinkedIn Insight Tag and your conversion pixels are installed and firing on any website.

## Detailed description
Stop guessing whether your LinkedIn Ads tracking works.

LinkedIn Insight Tag Checker inspects any page and tells you, in plain language:

• Whether the base Insight Tag is installed and **actually firing** — not just
  sitting in the HTML.
• The Partner ID(s) on the page, with a warning if a tag is duplicated.
• Every **event-specific conversion** that fires — its conversion ID, whether it
  was triggered by lintrk() or an image pixel, and whether the beacon reached
  LinkedIn. Click a button or submit a form with the popup open and watch the
  conversion appear live.

It also tells the truth when your own ad blocker or consent tool is blocking
LinkedIn's beacons, so you never mistake "blocked in my browser" for "broken on
the site."

Everything runs locally. No accounts, no data collection, nothing leaves your
browser.

Perfect for performance marketers, agencies, and web teams QA-ing LinkedIn Ads
conversion tracking.
